Symptoms That May Need Specialist Assessment

Persistent or concerning urinary and male reproductive symptoms should be discussed with an appropriate healthcare professional.

Urinary Symptoms

  • ✓ Blood in the urine
  • ✓ Burning or pain during urination
  • ✓ Frequent or urgent urination
  • ✓ Weak or interrupted urine flow
  • ✓ Pain in the side, back or lower abdomen
  • ✓ Difficulty emptying the bladder

Men's Urological Health

  • ✓ Prostate-related urinary problems
  • ✓ Male infertility concerns
  • ✓ Testicular lump or discomfort
  • ✓ Erectile or reproductive concerns
  • ✓ Scrotal or varicocele-related symptoms
  • ✓ Persistent male urinary symptoms
